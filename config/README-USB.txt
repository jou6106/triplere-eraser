Triple Re Eraser
================

Certified data erasure USB drive of Triple Re.

WARNING: booting a computer from this USB drive can permanently erase its disks.

- Erase reports (PDF) and logs are saved in the root of this drive and uploaded to
  the Triple Re ERP when the computer is online.
- etc/triplere/upload.conf : ERP key and licence (download it from the ERP).
- etc/nwipe/nwipe.conf     : company details printed on the reports.
